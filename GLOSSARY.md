# Portfolio

Personal portfolio for Ramesh Kumar: a single editorial homepage plus a resume page. All copy is content authored as data and shown on the site.

## Language

**Case study**:
An employer-scoped story on the homepage: organisation, role, and the outcomes delivered there.
_Avoid_: Experience, job, work history

**Project**:
A self-built or product-style piece of work shown on the homepage, always belonging to exactly one Section.
_Avoid_: Build (except in the Section name), app

**Section**:
A named grouping of Projects on the homepage. There are two: Selected Builds and Also ships UI.
_Avoid_: Tier, category, group

**Selected Builds**:
The primary Section: platform and backend work, shown with full ownership detail.

**Also ships UI**:
The secondary Section: full-stack product surfaces, shown in a quieter style and subordinate to Selected Builds.
_Avoid_: Secondary projects

**Proof points**:
The evidence shown on a Case study or Project: what was owned, the before and after, a Talking point, and supporting bullets. Any of them may be absent.
_Avoid_: Details, highlights

**Talking point**:
A question a reader could ask about the work, shown on the page to invite conversation.
_Avoid_: Interview question

**Motif**:
Decorative abstract artwork standing in for a photo on a Case study or Project. It carries no information and is hidden from assistive technology.
_Avoid_: Icon, graphic, illustration (as a specific term)

**Case visual**:
An informative diagram inside a Case study, such as an architecture sketch or a before/after chart. It has a text description and is read by assistive technology.
_Avoid_: Figure, chart, motif

**Visible**:
Appearing on the site. An item is either visible or hidden; there is no in-between state.
_Avoid_: Featured, enabled-but-hidden

**Writing**:
The latest posts from the external blog, linked from the homepage. Authored on the blog, not in this repo.
_Avoid_: Articles, blog section
